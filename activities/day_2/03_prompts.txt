Pull papers from here: https://proceedings.mlsys.org/paper_files/paper/2026. Start with ~20. 
Put them into a sample_papers folder in this repo.

Read ProfInfer and come up with 2 questions about the paper that could only be answered by reading the paper. 
One of them should use the specific terminology in the paper (i.e. so it could be found with a grep or something similar), one should use semantically similar terms but not the exact terms.
Write the questions so they can be asked without specific knowledge of which paper they are referring to.
Write them to questions.json with a 'question' field, an 'answer' field and a 'category' field that is either 'semantic' or 'lexical' and a `source_paper` field which is the name of the saved file.
Look at `03_long_context_benchmark.py` to see how these files will be used and should be formatted.
